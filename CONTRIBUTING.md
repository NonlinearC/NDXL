# Contributing to NDXL

Thank you for your interest in improving NDXL!

NDXL is an open-source project, and users are encouraged to report bugs, suggest improvements, request features, and discuss the library's existing implementations.

## Reporting Issues and Suggesting Improvements

The preferred way to contribute to NDXL is through GitHub Issues. When suggesting an improvement, please describe:

- The existing behaviour or implementation.
- The proposed change.
- Why the change would be beneficial.
- Any supporting evidence, such as benchmarks, examples, or references.

Code snippets and alternative implementations are welcome as supporting material.

**Pull requests are not accepted.** All changes to the official NDXL codebase are made by the project maintainer.

## Maintainer Review and Implementation

Suggestions and proposed improvements are reviewed at the maintainer's discretion and may be accepted, modified, postponed, or rejected.

Even when a suggestion is accepted, its implementation may differ from the original proposal. The maintainer independently evaluates, implements, and tests changes to ensure that they integrate correctly with the NDXL architecture and existing functionality.

The maintainer retains full discretion over how improvements are implemented, when they are released, and what changes are included in official versions.

Contributors whose suggestions lead to improvements will generally be acknowledged in the relevant release notes or documentation.

## Independent Modifications

The NDXL licence permits users to modify and redistribute their own copies of the software under its terms. Such modifications are independent of the official NDXL codebase and do not constitute official changes or releases.
